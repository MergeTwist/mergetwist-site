The Sixth Gift

A Christmas Eve thriller for iPhone and iPad. Episode 1 is free; episode 2 opens on 24 December at 20:47, episode 3 on 31 December at 20:47 (your local time).

Support

Purchase not showing on a new device? Open the game, go to the episode 2 screen and tap Restore purchase.

Anything else: write to the developer through the App Store page of the game.
